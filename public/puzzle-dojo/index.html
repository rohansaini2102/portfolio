Arrow Puzzle Dojo: Tap Maze

A minimalist arrow-tapping maze puzzle — sharpen your mind one tap at a time.

Tap the arrows to guide your way through each maze. Handcrafted levels ramp from a gentle warm-up to a proper brain workout, with a Daily Challenge, hints and star ratings. No account needed, and your progress is saved right on your device.
