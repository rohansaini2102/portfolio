Report an ad

Saw an ad that is offensive, misleading or not suitable for all ages?
Email rohansaini2102@gmail.com with the subject "Report an ad" and include:

  • a screenshot of the ad, or the advertiser's name
  • the time you saw it and your device (iPhone / Android)

I review every report and block the advertiser or category in the ad settings.
You can also tap the ⓘ or AdChoices icon on the ad itself.

Remove Ads and Restore Purchases

  • Remove Ads is a one-time purchase in Options. It removes banner and between-level ads.
  • New phone, or reinstalled? Open Options → Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play (play.google.com → Order history).

Privacy choices

  • In the game: Options → Privacy → Privacy choices.
  • iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads (delete or reset advertising ID).
  • Full details: Privacy Policy · Terms of Use

Common questions

  • Lost progress? Progress is saved on your device only. Uninstalling the game deletes it.
  • A level is too hard? Use a hint, or watch a short video for an extra hint or life.
  • Delete my data? Uninstall the game, reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings, and email me for anything else.
