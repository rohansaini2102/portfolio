Effective date: July 30, 2026

The short version

Arrow Puzzle Dojo: Tap Maze has no accounts, no ads, and no in-app purchases. We do not sell or share your data, and we collect nothing that identifies you personally.

The game is built with the Unity engine, and Unity's built-in engine diagnostics sends a small amount of technical information — including a device identifier — to Unity Technologies. That is the only data that leaves your device. Details are below.

What the app stores on your device

Your game progress (unlocked levels, star ratings, best times, daily-challenge streak, and the sound on/off setting) is saved only on your device, using the operating system's standard local app storage (iOS UserDefaults / Android SharedPreferences via Unity PlayerPrefs). This data:

  • never leaves your device;
  • is not accessible to us or to any third party;
  • is deleted automatically when you uninstall the app.

What is transmitted off your device

Unity engine diagnostics is enabled in this build. When the game starts, the Unity engine sends the following to Unity Technologies over an encrypted connection:

  • a Unity-generated device identifier;
  • device model, operating-system version, and hardware characteristics (processor, graphics, memory);
  • language and country setting;
  • app version and engine performance/stability information.

This information is used to diagnose crashes and performance problems. It is not linked to your name, email, or any account, it is not used for advertising or profiling, and neither we nor Unity sell it. We do not receive any personally identifying information from it.

Unity's handling of this data is governed by the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

What the app does NOT do

  • No name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or location is collected.
  • No advertising identifier is accessed; the app does not request the Android AD_ID permission.
  • No advertising SDKs are included and no ads are shown.
  • No third-party analytics or attribution SDKs are included.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties for their own purposes.

Children

The game is rated for all ages and contains no content directed at children under 13, no ads, and no social features. The Unity engine diagnostics described above applies to all users regardless of age. It collects no personal information and is not used for advertising, profiling, or targeting. If you are a parent or guardian and have questions about this, contact us at the address below.

Your rights (GDPR / CCPA and similar laws)

We hold no account, profile, or contact record for you, so we have nothing to export or correct. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored progress and stops all further diagnostics transmission.

For requests relating to data held by Unity Technologies, see the Unity privacy policy linked above, which describes how to contact Unity directly. You may also email us and we will help where we can.

Third-party services

The app is built with the Unity engine. Unity Analytics, Unity Ads, Unity Cloud Diagnostics, and Unity In-App Purchasing are all disabled in this app. Unity's built-in engine diagnostics is enabled, as described above.

Changes to this policy

If a future update adds features that change any of the above (for example, opt-in rewarded ads or a Remove Ads purchase), this policy will be updated first and the effective date revised.
