Privacy Policy: Arrow Puzzle Dojo: Tap Maze

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy explains what data is processed when you play Arrow Puzzle Dojo: Tap Maze ("the game") on Android or iOS. The game is made by Rohan Saini, an individual developer based in India ("I", "me").
Contact: rohansaini2102@gmail.com · 2nd Floor, VorkZone, JMD Megapolis, Sector 48, Gurugram, Haryana 122018, India

Summary

  • No account, no sign-in. I never ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your device.
  • The game is free because it shows ads. Google AdMob, the ad service, collects some device and usage data to show and measure ads. Personalised ads are only used where you allow them.
  • I don't use any analytics service. Apart from the ads, the only data that leaves your device is basic diagnostics from the Unity game engine.
  • Daily reminders are optional and are scheduled on your phone. No data is sent for them.
  • You can change your ad choices at any time in Options → Privacy → Privacy choices, and in your phone's settings.

1. Data stored only on your device

Level progress, stars, hearts, hints, sound, vibration and reminder settings, your Daily Challenge results and whether you bought "Remove Ads". This data is never sent to me. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

2. Data collected by services used in the game

2.1 Advertising: Google AdMob (Google LLC; Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland)

The game shows a banner during levels, a full-screen ad between some levels, and optional reward videos you can choose to watch for an extra life or hint.

The ads are served by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, AdMob collects:

  • device identifiers: the advertising ID (Android Advertising ID, or Apple's IDFA only if you allow tracking), the app set ID or IDFV, and similar identifiers;
  • IP address, used to estimate your general (approximate) location;
  • device and app information: model, operating system, language, app version;
  • ad interactions: which ads were shown, viewed or tapped, and video views;
  • diagnostic data: app and ad performance, crashes.

Purposes: to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and invalid traffic, and, only if you agree where consent is required, show personalised ads. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar device identifiers for this. Learn how Google uses this data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

If you don't consent to personalised ads, or you turn off tracking, you still see ads, but they are non-personalised or limited and are chosen using context such as the game itself.

2.2 Game engine diagnostics: Unity Technologies

The Unity game engine may send a Unity-generated device identifier together with device, operating-system and performance information, to keep the engine running well. Unity privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

2.3 Purchases: Google Play and Apple App Store

"Remove Ads" is bought through Google Play (or Apple on iOS). They process your payment under their own privacy policies. The game only receives confirmation that the purchase is valid and keeps a note on your device that you own it. I never see your card or account details.

2.4 Daily reminders (notifications)

If you turn on Daily reminders (in Options → Settings, or "Remind me" after a Daily Challenge), the game asks your phone for permission to show notifications and schedules at most one reminder a day, at 7 PM your local time. Reminders are created and stored on your device by Android or iOS. No data is sent to me or to anyone else for them. Turn them off any time in Options → Settings → Daily reminders, or in your phone's notification settings.

3. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Personalised ads, and storing or reading identifiers on your device for ads: your consent, collected through the consent form shown in the game.
  • Non-personalised ads, fraud prevention, engine diagnostics and keeping the game working: legitimate interests in running and funding a free game.
  • Delivering the Remove Ads purchase: performance of a contract.
  • Reminders: your choice to turn them on.

4. Your choices

  • In the game: Options → Privacy → Privacy choices reopens the consent form (EEA/UK/Switzerland) and the US-state privacy opt-out, where these apply.
  • iPhone / iPad: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking turns tracking off for this game.
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) lets you delete or reset your advertising ID.
  • Remove Ads (one-time purchase, from the No Ads button on Home or from Options) turns off banner ads and between-level ads. Optional reward videos stay available if you choose to watch them.
  • Reminders: Options → Settings → Daily reminders in the game, or your phone's notification settings.
  • Uninstalling the game deletes all data stored on your device.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK, Switzerland, California and other US states, or India), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, to data portability, to withdraw consent at any time, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal data for targeted advertising.

US states (including California): Showing personalised ads may count as "sharing" personal data for cross-context behavioural advertising. You can opt out in Options → Privacy → Privacy choices and with your device's ad settings. I do not sell personal data for money. I will not treat you differently for using your rights.

India (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023): You may ask for a summary of your data, its correction or erasure, and you may withdraw consent. You can complain to me first, and then to the Data Protection Board of India.

How to make a request: email rohansaini2102@gmail.com. I don't keep accounts, so I usually can't link data to you. Please say which device and platform you use. For data held by Google, you can also use Google's own tools. I reply within 30 days. EEA and UK users may complain to their local data protection authority.

6. Children

The game is a general-audience puzzle game. It is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA) and does not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has provided personal data, email me and I will help delete it.

7. How long data is kept

  • On your device: until you uninstall the game.
  • Google AdMob and Unity keep data according to their own policies, linked above.

8. Security and international transfers

All data sent by the game uses encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). I don't run my own servers. Google and Unity may process data in the United States and other countries, using safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, I will update it here and change the "Last updated" date. Important changes will also be noted in the game's update notes.
